gbemu is a Game Boy / Game Boy Color emulator written from scratch in Rust, compiled to WebAssembly — source on GitHub. No games are included or uploaded anywhere: ROMs are loaded and run locally in your browser. Bring your own homebrew — there are plenty of free games on Homebrew Hub (hh.gbdev.io) and itch.io. Battery saves and save states stay in this browser's local storage.
